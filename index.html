Your PLANA Match

Four questions. Sixteen planners scored against your answers with a weighted recommendation model — no accounts, no AI, no guessing. You will see exactly why it chose what it chose.

How the match works

An algorithm you can actually read

Nothing is hidden behind a black box — here is the whole model.

①

Every planner is scored

Each file declares a 0–5 weight for each possible answer: what it is for, which challenge it solves, which style it suits, and which rhythm it assumes.

②

Your answers are weighted

Purpose counts most (×1.3), then your biggest challenge (×1.15), then style (×1.0) and rhythm (×0.85) as the tie-breaker.

③

The result is explained

The winning score becomes your match percentage, and the reasons, sections, weekly structure and tips are generated from the same answers.

Our story

We make planners for the weeks that do not go to plan.

PLANA started in a university library at 2am, with a half-finished assignment and a planner that had been abandoned in week three. The problem was never discipline. It was that the planner assumed a life nobody actually lives.

What we believe

A planner should absorb a bad week, not punish it.

Most planning tools are designed for an idealised version of you: awake at 5am, never sick, never behind. Ours are designed for the real one. That means undated pages, restart spreads without guilt, rest planned as deliberately as work, and structures light enough to survive a week where everything slips.

Everything is made by two people, tested for a full semester before release, and updated for free forever.

Design that gets out of the way

Generous whitespace, one clear hierarchy per page, and no decoration that costs you a place to write.

Tested before it ships

Every file runs through a real semester or quarter with a small test group before it reaches the shop.

Priced like a tool, not a subscription

Buy once, own it, update forever. No accounts, no renewals, no held-back features.

FAQ

Questions worth answering

Which apps do the planners work in?
Every PDF planner is hyperlinked and tested in GoodNotes, Notability, Noteshelf and Xodo, and works in any standard PDF reader. Notion templates arrive as a duplicate link. Printables come in A4, A5 and US Letter.
Do I need an iPad?
No. The files work on Android tablets, Windows, macOS and paper. Only the handwriting experience differs — the structure is identical everywhere.
Are the planners dated?
Most are undated so you can start on any Tuesday in March without wasting two months of pages. The Calendar set ships both dated 2026 and undated versions.
What is your refund policy?
Digital files cannot be returned once downloaded, but if something does not work — a broken link, a layout problem, the wrong file — email us and we will fix it or refund you. Updates are always free.
Can I use PLANA files commercially?
Personal use is included with every purchase. For classroom, team or client use, email hello@plana.studio for an extended licence.
Is this a real shop?
No — PLANA is a fictional brand built as a front-end portfolio project using only HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The cart, checkout, recommendation engine and planner builder are all fully functional in the browser, but no payment is ever taken and no data leaves your device.
